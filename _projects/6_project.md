My doctoral research focused on computational modeling of metal additive manufacturing from powder delivery through solidification.

I developed coupled CFD–DEM models to quantify nozzle effects on powder flow in directed energy deposition, laser–particle interaction models for energy attenuation, and thermal/phase-field models for melt-pool and grain-structure prediction.

**Methods:** ANSYS Fluent, EDEM, COMSOL, FLOW-3D, Abaqus, Fortran, phase-field modeling.

**Applications:** powder-delivery optimization, laser-energy absorption, melt-pool prediction, heterogeneous nucleation, and grain-growth simulation.
