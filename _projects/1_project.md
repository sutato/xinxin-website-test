I develop a multiscale computational framework for lithium-ion battery electrode manufacturing that links particle-scale structure to continuum heat and mass transport during drying.

The workflow combines coarse-grained molecular dynamics, fluid flow, heat transfer, solvent diffusion, and microstructure reconstruction to predict how drying conditions generate binder gradients and heterogeneous electrode structures.

**Methods:** LAMMPS, COMSOL Multiphysics, particle-resolved modeling, heat and mass transfer, scientific computing.

**Applications:** drying-process optimization, binder-migration control, electrode quality prediction, and process–structure relationships.
