I develop a physics-informed surrogate framework for predicting grain-structure evolution in laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) of multicomponent alloys.

The framework couples CALPHAD thermodynamics, columnar-to-equiaxed transition criteria, phase-field simulation, and Gaussian-process classification/regression. It preserves key solidification physics while reducing expensive thermodynamic evaluations by roughly two orders of magnitude.

**Methods:** Fortran, MATLAB, Python, CALPHAD, phase-field modeling, Gaussian processes, uncertainty quantification.

**Program context:** DARPA SURGE collaboration with Texas A&M University and AlphaSTAR.
