I developed coarse-grained molecular dynamics models to investigate particle rearrangement, carbon-binder-domain evolution, and interfacial debonding during cathode electrode drying.

The work provides a particle-resolved route for connecting slurry composition and drying conditions to the final electrode skeleton and mechanical integrity.

**Methods:** LAMMPS, coarse-grained molecular dynamics, particle interactions, microstructure analysis.

**Industry context:** battery-manufacturing research conducted with General Motors Global R&D.
