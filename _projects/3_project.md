I build multiphysics models to understand and improve solid-state battery performance and manufacturing.

Projects include modeling how conductive alignment enhances cathode utilization and developing ultrasonic-processing models to reveal healing mechanisms and guide process conditions.

**Methods:** COMSOL Multiphysics, electrochemical transport, electric-field modeling, mechanics, heat transfer.

**Research goal:** connect processing conditions and mesoscale structure to electrochemical performance and reliability.
