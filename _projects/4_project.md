I developed a coupled FFT-based crystal-plasticity and phase-field framework to simulate recrystallization in gradient-grained metals.

The model transfers deformation information and geometrically necessary dislocation fields from the crystal-plasticity solver into the phase-field model to study nucleation, grain-boundary migration, and microstructure evolution.

**Methods:** Fortran, MATLAB, FFT-based crystal plasticity, phase-field modeling, parallel computing.

**Applications:** recrystallization, gradient-grained materials, process–microstructure relationships, and deformation-driven microstructure evolution.
